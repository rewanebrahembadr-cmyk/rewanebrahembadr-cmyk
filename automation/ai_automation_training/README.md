# AI & Automation Training

A practical adult-learning track focused on understanding, designing, building, evaluating, and deploying AI-powered automation systems.

## Learning Philosophy

Think → Design → Build → Connect → Automate → Evaluate → Deploy

The track is intentionally designed around transferable engineering thinking rather than a single tool. n8n is used as a major implementation environment, while the curriculum also covers APIs, retrieval systems, AI agents, MCP, deployment, reliability, and business solution design.

## Current Curriculum Structure

1. AI Systems Primer
2. LLM Interaction Design
3. Knowledge & Retrieval Systems
4. Workflow Engineering
5. Intelligent Automation
6. Agentic Systems
7. Production & Reliability
8. Applied AI Solutions
9. AI Solution Strategy
10. Capstone — Build to Deploy
